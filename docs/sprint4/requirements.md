# Sprint 4 requirements snapshot

Sprint 4 follows the supplied “Batter Recognition Skill” direction. It uses the immutable accepted Sprint 3 snapshot from 2026-03-25 through 2026-09-09 and retains the 10,755 legal offensive recognition opportunities and 2,112 recognized outcomes.

The implementation measures recognition relative to a contextual expectation model without batter identity, uses partial pooling and uncertainty, preserves all 602 batter records before support filters, holds publication and ranking thresholds at 20 and 30 opportunities, tests historical effects only on future games, separates recognition from accuracy and aggressiveness, runs fixed sensitivities and negative controls, and publishes rankings only if every preregistered gate passes.
