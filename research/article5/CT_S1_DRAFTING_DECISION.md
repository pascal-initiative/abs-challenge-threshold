# CT-S1 Numerical Drafting Decision

## Decision

**Numerical drafting may begin for Challenge Threshold Standard 1 (CT-S1).**

The article and companion methodology may describe the versioned named
reference evaluator, its temporal validation, its support boundary, the one
eligible ordinary count contrast, separate conditional sampling intervals, and
CT Model Spread. Every number must come from an `APPROVED_RESTRICTED` entry in
`CT_S1_PUBLIC_CLAIMS.json`.

## Required public interpretation

Challenge Threshold is the confidence a challenger would need, under the named
fixed-path value model, for a challenge to break even. It does not estimate what
a player saw or believed. CT-S1 is a reproducible standardization choice, not a
uniquely correct or optimal strategy.

Affected point values must display the range across the eight chosen modeling
conventions. The 156 supported rows per period without a complete spread may
not appear as bare point estimates. Sampling intervals must remain separate
from model spread, and the surviving contrast has no paired sampling interval
for its difference.

## Not authorized

No Pascal ABS Challenge Playbook, challenge/preserve action bands, universal
count rule, optimal-policy claim, player-decision grade, or `CT-2026` relabeling
is authorized. Publication, graphics, site implementation, and distribution
remain separate owner-controlled gates.

Prepare the first numerical findings outline and companion-methodology outline
from the restricted registry. Fact and claim review must pass before polished
