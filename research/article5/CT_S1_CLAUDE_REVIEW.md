   was calculated for the difference.

7. Keep "one of three" beside the surviving ordinary representative and show
   the two failed representatives in the article or companion methodology.
8. Make explicit that the trailing score state is not win-probability leverage
   in the fixed-path expected-runs model.
9. Explain that temporal stability of the named convention is not model
   validity or agreement among conventions.
10. Define CT Model Spread as the range across eight chosen conventions, not a
    distribution or an interval with coverage probability.

The formal article definition should say "the confidence a challenger would
need under the named model for the challenge to break even." The shorter
locked line—"Challenge Threshold tells you how sure you need to be; it does not
tell you how sure you are"—may remain a clearly explained editorial shorthand,
not player-specific advice.

## Strongest counterargument preserved

only surviving ordinary representative difference is smaller than the
convention-driven spread in level. CT-S1 point values therefore substantially
encode a chosen standard, and the name "Standard" may carry more authority
than caveats can contain. The article must confront that argument directly.
